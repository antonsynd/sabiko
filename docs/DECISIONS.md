whether it is a one-way or two-way door. Dates are when the owner settled them.

| 52 | 2026-10-29 | Tuples and `()` values (#6). | 53 | 2026-10-29 | Methods (#7). An inherent `impl` block is allowed for a struct of the file only (E0211 otherwise, rustc's E0390); its members are functions, with an optional first `self`, `mut self`, `&self` or `&mut self`. They are definitions outside the item namespace, named `Type::Name` (exactly two segments; `Self::Name` and `Self` inside the block) or called with method syntax; fields and methods are separate namespaces. A method call finds its method as rustc's probe does for inherent impls: the receiver is dereferenced step by step and each step is matched by value, then by `&`, then by `&mut` (a reference matched by value is reborrowed); a path call (`P::Get(p)`) adjusts nothing.
